God, !!there are some hard parts of my heart that need to be broken up.!!
!!help me!!.
i want to sow seeds of faith, hope, and love, not anger, resentment, and bitterness.
i surrender those hard parts to You!
today, i want to walk in Your righteousness and be covered by Your blessings.
in Jesus' name, amen.
