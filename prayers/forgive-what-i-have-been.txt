most merciful God,
Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,
i confess that i have sinned in thought, word, and deed.
i have not loved You with my whole heart.
i have not loved my neighbors as myself.
in Your mercy, forgive what i have been.
!!help me to amend what i am and direct what i shall be!!.
that i may do justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with You, my God.
in Jesus' name. amen.
