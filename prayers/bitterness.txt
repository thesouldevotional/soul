dear God,
today i remember how destructive bitterness can be in my life,
especially if i leave it unchecked.
although the wounds i have received are real and painful,
forgive me for where i have allowed bitterness to take root.
forgive me for the times i have ranted, gossiped, and seethed rather than forgiven.
i pause now and bring to mind those people and situations i feel resentful about.
as i think of them, !!i bring each one humbly before You. cleanse my heart!!.
help me get rid of all bitterness so that i can display kindness, love, and grace toward others.
make me a channel of Your unending love and overflowing forgiveness, today and every day.
in Jesus' name, amen.
