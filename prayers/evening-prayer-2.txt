Lord, thank You.
thank You for a home that shelters me from the wind and the rain.
i have food, i have people who love me.
even when there are tears, i am still grateful.

Lord, i place myself and my family into Your hands.
day and night, please protect my family, and keep them far from all evil.
i do not know what tomorrow will bring, but i am not afraid,
because You promised:
\ "don't be afraid, for I am with you.
\ don't be discouraged, for I am your God.
\ I will strengthen you and help you.
\ I will hold you up with My victorious right hand."
\ — [Isaiah 41:10](post.html?p=bible/isaiah_41-10.txt)

Lord, please strengthen my spirit.
renew my faith and fill my heart with hope.

Lord, thank You for forgiving all my sins.
thank You for taking away my fear, my worries and my anxiety.
cleanse my heart with Your word, and send Your angels to protect me.
!!let me sleep peacefully, calmly and deeply!!,
and when i wake up tomorrow, let my heart be filled with gratitude and peace.

Lord, bless the people i love and their families too.
let them sleep like children, peacefully and safely,
and when they wake up tomorrow, may their minds be clear, far from all evil and temptation.
amen.
