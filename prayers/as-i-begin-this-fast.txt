fasting | as i begin this fast

↳ en // [it](https://thesouldevotional.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=prayers/mentre-inizio-questo-digiuno.txt)

Father, as i begin this fast, i set this time apart for You.
i'm not trying to earn anything, i just want to make room.
before anything else, i confess my sins to You and i turn back to You.
Holy Spirit, show me the places where i'm weak, even the ones i'd rather not see.
i forgive everyone who has hurt me — give me the humility to ask forgiveness of those i have hurt.
i let go of every worldly desire that holds me back, so i can surrender my whole life to Jesus.
!!every time i feel hungry, turn my attention back to You!!.
humble me, quiet the noise, and show me what You want me to see.
let this stay between You and me, not something to show.
amen.
