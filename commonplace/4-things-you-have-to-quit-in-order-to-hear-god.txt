4 things you have to quit in order to hear God

↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://thesouldevotional.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=commonplace/4-cose-che-devi-smettere-di-fare-per-sentire-dio.txt)

\ i used to beg God to speak to me — tell me what to do, give me a sign — and every time it felt like silence. turns out He'd been speaking the whole time; i just had too much noise blocking the signal. four things i quit so i could finally hear Him:
\
\ 1. filling every silence with my phone — no podcast in the car, no scroll while waiting in line. if you can't sit with silence for five minutes, you can't hear from a God who specializes in whispers.
\ 2. asking everyone else before i ask God — friends, reddit, google, until i'd already been shaped by seventeen opinions before i ever prayed.
\ 3. only praying and never listening — prayer isn't a monologue, it's a conversation. now every prayer ends with five minutes of silence, listening.
\ 4. dismissing the gentle nudge as just my own thoughts — most of the time God doesn't sound like a thunderclap, He sounds like a whisper.
\
\ !!the moment i started removing the noise, i realized God had been speaking the entire time — i just kept calling it my own thoughts.!!
