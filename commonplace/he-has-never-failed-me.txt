He has never failed me
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://thesouldevotional.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=commonplace/non-mi-ha-mai-deluso.txt)

\ worry speaks louder than memory.
\ it fills the evening with everything that could go wrong tomorrow
\ and quietly skips over everything that has already gone right.
\
\ !!i worry far too much for someone God has never once let down!!.
\ when i look back honestly, i can't find a single time He wasn't there.
\ some doors closed, and later i understood why;
\ the things i was sure would break me didn't.
\ His faithful love has not run out on me once ([Lamentations 3:22](post.html?p=bible/lamentations_3-22-23.txt#v22)).
\
\ and worrying doesn't add anything.
\ Jesus asked whether all our worries can add a single moment to our life ([Matthew 6:27](post.html?p=bible/matthew_6-19-34.txt#v27) *),
\ and pointed at the birds, fed without storing anything in barns.
\ worry doesn't change tomorrow. it just takes today away from me.
\
\ what Scripture asks for instead is to hand it over:
\ "give all your worries and cares to God, for He cares about you" ([1 Peter 5:7](post.html?p=bible/1-peter_5-6-8.txt#v7) *),
\ and to pray about everything, instead of turning it over and over in my head ([Philippians 4:6](post.html?p=bible/philippians_4-4-8.txt#v6) *).
\
\ so when the old fear starts up again, there's one question to ask:
\ !!when has He ever failed me?!!
\ the honest answer is never.
