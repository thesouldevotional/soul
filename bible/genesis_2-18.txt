[18] then the Lord God said, "!!it is not good for the man to be alone!!.
I will make a helper who is just right for Him."

---

God sees that something in creation isn't complete yet: the man is alone. when we read "helper", it's easy to fall into a modern trap and hear it almost as a biblical stamp of approval on patriarchy. it isn't. the hebrew word, ezer, doesn't mean someone subordinate: in the bible it's used most often for God Himself, who comes to help and support His people ([Psalms 121:1-4](post.html?p=bible/psalms_121-1-4.txt)). and "just right for Him" literally means "in front of Him", "corresponding to Him": not behind, beside. it's natural, then, to read in this verse the relational nature of human beings, who find their completeness in relationship with others.
