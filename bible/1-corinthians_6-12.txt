[12] you say, "i am allowed to do anything"—but not everything is good for you.
and even though "i am allowed to do anything," !!I must not become a slave to anything!!.

---

we think we know what freedom is.
some even like to say they don't believe in God, but if you look closely, all the freedom they claim to have is just worshipping another idol: money, success, themselves.
that's not real freedom. we can be free in God and believing in Jesus.
when our only purpose is to rest in God and live by His word, we're free from people's judgement, we are truly allowed to be ourselves.
sometimes the big misconception is that as christians we have to follow a ton of rules in order to be loved and forgiven. it's the complete opposite.
Jesus became human and died on the cross because He knew we are sinners, we are forgiven thanks to His immense sacrifice.
He loves us as we are, sins and mistakes included. and you already know why it is hard to accept and believe. because we can't love ourselves in our imperfections. we always think we have to deserve it. it's hard to believe someone loves us more than we love ourselves.
but God does. Jesus died because He does. and this is the real freedom we've been gifted.
