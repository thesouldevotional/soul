[13] Jesus replied, "anyone who drinks this water will soon become thirsty again.
[14] but !!those who drink the water I give will never be thirsty again!!.
it becomes a fresh, bubbling spring within them, giving them eternal life."

---

water symbolism, Jesus talking to a Samaritan woman, immoral, despised.
the typical person Jesus would talk to.
what Jesus offers is eternal satisfaction through the Word, through !!living water!!.
and said to that woman, it means that no matter our past, we can all feel safe coming to Jesus.
