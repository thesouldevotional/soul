[14] the Lord replied,
"!!I will personally go with you!!, Moses,
and I will give you rest—everything will be fine for you."

---

this comes right after the golden calf. the people broke the covenant almost as soon as they received it, and God said He would send an angel ahead of them but would not travel with them Himself. Moses pleads with Him, and God grants it: He will go with them in person.

"everything will be fine" sounds like what we say when we have nothing better to offer. here it's a promise, and the same God who fought for them at the sea ([Exodus 14:14](post.html?p=bible/exodus_14-14.txt) *) now walks beside them.
the road won't get easier: forty years of desert are still ahead. the rest He promises is in who walks it with them.
