[9] the Lord God made all sorts of trees grow up from the ground—
!!trees that were beautiful and that produced delicious fruit!!.
in the middle of the garden He placed the tree of life
and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.

---

the description of Eden. God's generosity in creation: it isn't made only to sustain us, it's made to be beautiful too, to feed the spirit as well. at the center there's the gift of life, the tree of life, and there's the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. that second tree is the boundary love needs in order to be real: nothing is authentic if there's no real alternative to it. without the possibility of choosing, for example, there's no obedience. God's invitation, later betrayed ([Genesis 3:1-15](post.html?p=bible/genesis_3-1-15.txt) *), was to trust His wisdom instead of deciding for ourselves what is right and what is wrong.
