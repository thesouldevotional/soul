[10] obviously, i'm !!not trying to win the approval of people, but of God!!.
if pleasing people were my goal, i would not be Christ's servant.

~ ↳ source: [anthology-lab / book note / the practice of the presence of God](https://anthology-lab.github.io/books/the-practice-of-the-presence-of-god-lawrence.html)

---
Brother Lawrence spent fifteen years in a monastery kitchen, a job he didn't like.
he said the best way he had found to go to God was "doing our common business without any view of pleasing men", purely for the love of God.
it's Paul's line, moved into a kitchen.
most of what i do all day, someone sees it, judges it, approves it or not.
and i notice. i adjust, a little, without deciding to.
the real question is who i'm doing it for while i do it.
washing a pot for God is the same pot. what changes is where my attention goes.
when i catch myself working for the look on someone's face, i don't need to fight it.
i turn back, the way he did, and keep going.
