[14] God replied to Moses, "!!I AM WHO I AM!!.
say this to the people of Israel: I AM has sent me to you."

---
\ when Moses asks God's name, the answer is "I AM WHO I AM".
\ not a name in the usual sense, a name that just states existence itself.
\ Aquinas builds His whole doctrine of God on that one line: God isn't a being among beings, competing for space with everything else that exists, God is the act of existing itself, the reason anything is there to be counted at all.
\ it reframes the question people still ask today ("does God exist?") as a category error — existence isn't a property God happens to have, it's what the word God means.
