1 Peter 5:6-8 | give your worries to Him

[6] so humble yourselves under the mighty power of God,
and at the right time He will lift you up in honor.
[7] give all your worries and cares to God,
for !!He cares about you!!.
[8] stay alert! watch out for your great enemy, the devil.
he prowls around like a roaring lion, looking for someone to devour.

~ ↳ source: [janelle.everywhere iga](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbSsyzjyVKS/)

---

overthinking feels productive, like turning a worry over one more time will finally produce a plan. mostly it just produces more worry. Peter doesn't tell you to stop caring or pretend the problem isn't there — He tells you where to put it: give all your worries and cares to God.

that instruction only makes sense because of what follows it. !!He cares about you!! isn't a throwaway line, it's the reason handing it over is safe. you don't give something to someone who's indifferent — you give it to someone paying attention, capable of carrying what you can't.

so the discipline isn't never worrying again. it's noticing the spiral start and choosing, again, to hand it over instead of carrying it alone.
