[11] !!i have hidden Your word in my heart!!,
that i might not sin against You.
[15] i will study Your commandments
and reflect on Your ways.

---

it is important to read the bible, to study it. it is the way God talked to us.
when the Word is in our hearts, that's when we can live our life really trying to apply it.
it becomes part of us.
so let's not study the bible as a science textbook, with our brains only.
let it sink deep, and hide it in our hearts.
