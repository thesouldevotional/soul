[1] !!the Lord is My light and My salvation —
so why should I be afraid?!!
the Lord is My fortress, protecting Me from danger,
so why should I tremble?

---

God protects us from evil, even when we can't see it. !!it's not only about feeling safe, it's about abiding, knowing God!!.
we have to trust in God's timing and goodness, and we have to accept that of course we can't see things like He does.
