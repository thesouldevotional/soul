Matthew 7:24-27 | the wise and foolish builders

[24] "anyone who listens to My teaching and follows it is wise, !!like a person who builds a house on solid rock!!.
[25] though the rain comes in torrents and the floodwaters rise and the winds beat against that house, it won't collapse because it is built on bedrock.
[26] but anyone who hears My teaching and doesn't obey it is foolish, like a person who builds a house on sand.
[27] when the rains and floods come and the winds beat against that house, it will collapse with a mighty crash."

→ also in

Luke 6:47-49
[47] I will show you what it's like when someone comes to Me, listens to My teaching, and then follows it.
[48] it is like a person building a house who digs deep and lays the foundation on solid rock. when the floodwaters rise and break against that house, it stands firm because it is well built.
[49] but anyone who hears and doesn't obey is like a person who builds a house right on the ground, without a foundation. when the floods sweep down against that house, it will collapse into a heap of ruins."

---

during the [Sermon on the Mount](post.html?p=bible/matthew_5-1-7-29.txt) * Jesus says these words.
it's hard sometimes to come to terms with the fact that our God never promises to spare us from all suffering, because sometimes for us that would be easier. but He never does. He does promise, though, to be by our side always, even in times of trouble. in the Sermon Jesus tells us how to try and live a righteous life, and He promises that if we abide in Him in that way, that's the solid foundation that will allow us to live a life that won't be shaken even during adversities.
every decision we make is a statement: are we building on top of the ever changing rules of this world, or Jesus' Word?
