[18] i am making this covenant with you so that no one among you—no man, woman, clan, or tribe—
will turn away from the Lord our God to worship these gods of other nations,
and so that !!no root among you bears bitter and poisonous fruit!!.

---

this is the verse behind [Hebrews 12:15](post.html?p=bible/hebrews_12-14-17.txt#v15): "watch out that no poisonous root of bitterness grows up to trouble you". the first readers of Hebrews heard Moses in those words.

bitterness is a root, not a wound. a wound is in plain sight and heals in the open. a root grows underground for years, and nobody sees it until it comes up somewhere else: in my tone of voice, in a friendship i quietly undermine, in a resentment that makes choices for me.

and it doesn't stay with me. it poisons the people around me too.

there's a strange pleasure in it: venting, going over the wrong one more time. the pleasure doesn't make it safe to keep.

the warning in Hebrews is written to the whole community, in the plural. i'm not meant to dig the root out alone. i bring it to God honestly, and to the people who will walk toward forgiveness with me.
