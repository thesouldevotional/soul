[21] your own ears will hear Him.
right behind you a voice will say,
"!!this is the way you should go!!,"
whether to the right or to the left.

---

it's hard sometimes to accept that God is inside of us, in our hearts. if we listen, if our heart is open and we let the Holy Spirit guide us, we will hear Him, He will guide us.
