[1] !!I love You, Lord; You are My strength!!.
[2] the Lord is My rock, My fortress, and My savior;
My God is My rock, in whom I find protection.
He is My shield, the power that saves Me,
and My place of safety.

---

you don't pile up seven different words for "shelter" unless you have actually needed a place to hide. these verses aren't poetry: David is remembering years spent hiding in desert caves when He had nowhere else to run.
He also repeats "My". it's one thing to believe God is strong in general, and !!another to discover that His strength belongs personally to you when your own runs out!!.
