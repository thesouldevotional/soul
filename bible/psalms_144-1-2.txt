Psalms 144:1-2 | the Lord, who is My rock

[1] praise the Lord, who is My rock.
He trains My hands for war and gives My fingers skill for battle.
[2] !!He is My loving ally and My fortress, My tower of safety, My rescuer!!.
He is My shield, and I take refuge in Him.
He makes the nations submit to Me.

---

this verse is part of a three-part formation. [Psalm 91](post.html?p=bible/psalms_91-1-2.txt) * is the defensive front — protection over you, your family, your home, your mindset. [Psalm 20](post.html?p=bible/psalms_20-4-7.txt) * is the offensive front — sung before the battle, declaring victory in advance because the outcome already rests with God. Psalm 144 is the sword — hands trained for war, ready before the fight begins. together, they cover what's needed to stand: shelter first, then a shout, then a weapon in hand.
to make it your own, read the [15 declarations from Psalm 144](post.html?p=commonplace/15-declarations-from-psalm-144.txt): the psalm rewritten in the first person, to say out loud.
