[14] the Lord !!Himself will fight for you!!.
just stay calm.

---

many in the bible found themselves facing trials that looked impossible. Moses and His people are trapped: the sea in front of them, Pharaoh's army closing in behind. and still God provides.

"just stay calm" is one of the sentences that annoy us most. nobody has ever felt calmer because somebody told them to.
but here it's God saying it. we're asked to trust Him, and He will save us. He will even fight for us.

staying calm doesn't mean standing still, though: in the very next verse He tells Moses to get the people moving.
