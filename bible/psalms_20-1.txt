[1] in times of trouble, may the Lord answer your cry.
may the name of the God of Jacob !!keep you safe from all harm!!.

---

this verse takes into account that hard days will come ([Job 1:20-22](post.html?p=bible/job_1-20-22.txt) *), and asks for God's help. calling on "the God of Jacob" is a quiet reminder that your safety doesn't rest on a spotless track record, but !!on God's fierce loyalty to flawed, anxious people!!—lifting you onto high ground far above whatever is chasing you.
