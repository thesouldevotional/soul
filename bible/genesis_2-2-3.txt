[2] on the seventh day God had finished His work of creation,
!!so He rested from all His work!!.
[3] and God blessed the seventh day and declared it holy,
because it was the day when He rested from all His work of creation.

---

this is where every reflection on rest begins. God doesn't rest because He's tired, He rests because the work is finished. and the moment matters for another reason too: Adam, created on the sixth day, lives His first full day on the seventh. as if to say that we start from rest, from living in God's grace, and work from there. in a wider sense, it's also a reminder against hustle culture, against the idea that we have to be productive 24/7. we don't. we always need to rest, and resting isn't idling: it's truly living in God's grace.
