Philippians 3:20-21 | where we truly belong

[20] but !!we are citizens of Heaven!!, where the Lord Jesus Christ lives.
and we are eagerly waiting for Him to return as our Savior.
[21] He will take our weak mortal bodies and change them into glorious bodies like His own,
using the same power with which He will bring everything under His control.

---

we spend years looking for a place that fits: a city, a group of friends, a job, a person.
some of them hold us for a while. then something shifts and we feel out of place again.
that restlessness has a reason: !!we were made for a home this world can't give!!.
our citizenship is already written in Heaven, where Christ is.
this frees us to live fully here, to love the people and places we've been given without asking them to be everything.
even this tired body is part of the promise.
and we wait, the way you wait for someone you love to come home.
