[6] so be strong and courageous!
do not be afraid and do not panic before them.
for the Lord your God will personally go ahead of you.
!!He will neither fail you nor abandon you!!.

---

when you stand at the edge of something overwhelming, your first instinct is to measure your own strength—and usually, it feels like it won't be enough.
Moses doesn't tell the Israelites to be brave because they are strong enough to handle what's ahead. !!He tells them to be brave because they aren't going alone!!.
real courage isn't the absence of fear; it's the quiet certainty that even when familiar support fades and the road looks daunting, God refuses to let go of your hand.
