2 Timothy 3:16-17 | equipped for every good work

↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

[16] all Scripture is inspired by God and is useful to teach us what is true and to make us realize what is wrong in our lives.
!!it corrects us when we are wrong and teaches us to do what is right!!.
[17] God uses it to prepare and equip His people to do every good work.

---

i keep expecting a voice from the sky. it's already been written down.
Scripture isn't decoration — it teaches, it corrects, it trains me toward what's right.
when i feel lost, the answer isn't silence. it's a book i haven't opened in a while.
God speaks. He's just not shouting over the noise i keep making.

reading it is half of it. it changes me when i live what it says.
some days it corrects a thought i was sure of. some days it shows me something about Him i hadn't seen.
slowly, what i want changes, and so does what i look at.
verse 17 says what it's for: to prepare me for the good work God already has in mind. this is how He equips me for the life He planned.
