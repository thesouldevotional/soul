[3] yes, He humbled you by letting you go hungry and then feeding you with manna,
a food previously unknown to you and your ancestors.
!!He did it to teach you that people do not live by bread alone;
rather, we live by every word that comes from the mouth of the Lord!!.

---

"we do not live by bread alone". when i talk to non-believers i usually quote this verse, because it's so simple yet it's something so important. there's more, !!there's a lot more to aim for in life than the basics, than materialism!!. if we trust in God, He will provide for us.
