[8] You keep track of all My sorrows.
!!You have collected all My tears in Your bottle.!!
You have recorded each one in Your book.

---

this verse comes from a psalm David wrote after the Philistines seized Him in Gath — captured, alone, far from anyone who could rescue Him. in that fear, He doesn't say God removed the tears. He says God is collecting them, one by one, in a bottle, and writing each one down. the sorrow isn't erased, it's witnessed — kept safe as proof that nothing you cry is unseen or unrecorded.
