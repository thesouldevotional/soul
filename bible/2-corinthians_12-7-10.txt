2 Corinthians 12:7-10 | My power works best in weakness
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://thesouldevotional.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=bible/2-corinthians_12-7-10.txt)

[7] even though I have received such wonderful revelations from God.
so to keep Me from becoming proud, I was given a thorn in My flesh, a messenger from Satan to torment Me and keep Me from becoming proud.
[8] three different times I begged the Lord to take it away.
[9] each time He said, "!!My grace is all you need. My power works best in weakness.!!"
so now I am glad to boast about My weaknesses, so that the power of Christ can work through Me.
[10] !!that's why I take pleasure in My weaknesses, and in the insults, hardships, persecutions, and troubles that I suffer for Christ.
for when I am weak, then I am strong.!!

~ ↳ source: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorn_in_the_flesh)

---

Paul never says what the thorn was.
over the centuries people have guessed almost everything:
a physical illness, the persecutions and hardships He went through,
the opponents who kept undermining His work,
His grief over His own people rejecting the gospel, a temptation He couldn't shake off.
some scholars think it simply can't be identified from what we have;
others read it more literally, as the messenger from Satan He names right after.
the phrase passed into everyday english for a trouble that keeps coming back and won't go away.

maybe it's better not to know.
if He had named it, we'd read this as the story of His problem.
left unnamed, it fits whatever each of us carries.

what is clear is how it went.
He asked three times, and the thorn stayed.
He got grace enough to live with it, and a strength that shows up right where He was weakest.
He even says why: so that He wouldn't become proud after everything He had been shown.
praying to be healed is right.
so is going on when healing doesn't come.
