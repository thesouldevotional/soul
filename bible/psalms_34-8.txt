[8] !!taste and see that the Lord is good!!.
oh, the joys of those who take refuge in Him!

---

an invitation to !!move from secondhand belief to firsthand experience!!, urging us not merely to analyze or agree with God's goodness from a distance, but to personally test and experience it in the middle of real trouble.
