[11] no discipline is enjoyable while it is happening—it's painful!
but !!afterward there will be a peaceful harvest of right living!!
for those who are trained in this way.

---

in many verses the bible reminds us that we have to think long-term, and it's important. even "long-term" is an understatement: with God we should be thinking about eternity. we're not to do anything to please people, to achieve earthly desires. everything we do is for God, to praise Him, to live in His Word. how much this changes everything. how much this helps to see discipline even when it's hard.
