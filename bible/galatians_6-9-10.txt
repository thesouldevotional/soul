Galatians 6:9-10 | don't grow weary of doing good
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://thesouldevotional.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=bible/galatians_6-9-10.txt)

[9] so let's not get tired of doing what is good.
!!at just the right time we will reap a harvest of blessing if we don't give up.!!
[10] therefore, whenever we have the opportunity, we should do good to everyone—especially to those in the family of faith.

---

the hardest part of caring for your body was never the first day, it's the thousandth.
the workout that stops mattering, the meal that stops feeling like a choice, the early night that stops feeling like sacrifice.
that's exactly where Paul says don't give up.
the harvest comes at the right time, not on your timeline.
a healthier body isn't built in a week, it's built in not quitting.
