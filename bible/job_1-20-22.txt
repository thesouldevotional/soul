[20] Job stood up and tore His robe in grief.
then He shaved His head and fell to the ground to worship.
[21] He said,
"I came naked from My mother's womb,
and I will be naked when I leave.
the Lord gave Me what I had,
and the Lord has taken it away.
!!praise the name of the Lord!!!"
[22] in all of this, Job did not sin by blaming God.

---

Job is grieving, He lost everything. still He's blessing God.
do we follow God only if He serves us the way we want Him to, or can we be like Job, and !!follow His plan, no matter what!!?
