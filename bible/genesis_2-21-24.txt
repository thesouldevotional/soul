[21] so the Lord God caused the man to fall into a deep sleep.
while the man slept, the Lord God took out one of the man's ribs and closed up the opening.
[22] then the Lord God made a woman from the rib, and He brought Her to the man.
[23] "at last!" the man exclaimed.
"!!this one is bone from My bone,
and flesh from My flesh!!!
She will be called 'woman,'
because She was taken from 'man.'"
[24] this explains why a man leaves his father and mother and is joined to his wife,
and the two are united into one.

---

in [Genesis 2:18](post.html?p=bible/genesis_2-18.txt) * God says it isn't good for the man to be alone, and promises a helper "just right for Him". here is the answer. Adam has looked at all of creation without finding anyone to share His life with. now there is someone. "at last!": the first words a human being speaks in the bible are a cry of joy in front of another person. She is taken from His side, neither above Him nor beneath Him: bone from His bone, His equal, the one who stands in front of Him and corresponds to Him. and the two become one: this is where marriage begins. we were made for relationship, for a communion that mirrors God's own.
