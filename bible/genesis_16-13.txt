[13] thereafter, Hagar used another name to refer to the Lord, who had spoken to her.
she said, "!!You are the God who sees me!!."
she also said, "have i truly seen the One who sees me?"

~ ↳ source: [janelle.everywhere iga](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbSsyzjyVKS/)

---

Hagar is the first person in the bible to give God a name: "the God who sees me". she is a woman, a slave, a fugitive in the desert. it shows that God never forgets the "invisible" ones: He goes looking for them, listens to them, gives them dignity and, in the end, hope.
