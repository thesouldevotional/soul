[20] you intended to harm Me, but !!God intended it all for good!!.
He brought Me to this position so I could save the lives of many people.

---

Joseph's brothers sold Him as a slave, but years later He doesn't react the way we'd find humanly understandable, with rage. He doesn't play down what His brothers did, but He recognizes that in the end what matters is God's plan, and that God used even His suffering to carry it out. so powerful.
