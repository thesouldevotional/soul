[4] "listen, o Israel! the Lord is our God, the Lord alone.
[5] and !!you must love the Lord your God with all your heart, all your soul, and all your strength!!.
[6] and you must commit yourselves wholeheartedly to these commands that I am giving you today.
[7] repeat them again and again to your children.
talk about them when you are at home and when you are on the road,
when you are going to bed and when you are getting up.
[8] tie them to your hands and wear them on your forehead as reminders.
[9] write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates.

---

these verses build on the commandments.
it's in the end as simple as that: !!love God with all your being, heart, mind and soul!!.
and it's not a private commitment, it's an invitation to share it, to live it in community.
