[11] for the honor of Your name, O Lord,
forgive My many, many sins.

---

when we apologize to people, we instinctively try to shrink the offense: "it wasn't that bad".
David does the exact opposite, asking God to forgive His guilt precisely because it is great, offering zero excuses and appealing solely to God's own character ("for the honor of Your name").
you never have to downplay your failure to make it small enough for grace; the bigger the mess, !!the more clearly God's mercy reveals who He really is!!.
