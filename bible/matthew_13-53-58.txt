---

at first they were amazed. in Luke they speak well of Him, struck by His gracious words.
then familiarity kicks in. we know Him. He's the carpenter's son. we know His mother, His brothers.
the doubt didn't come from anything He said. it came from what they thought they already knew.
familiarity turned amazement into offense in a few minutes. in Luke they try to throw Him off a cliff.

familiarity is my risk too.
i've heard these stories since i was a child. i know how they end.
the words lose their weight because i've heard them too many times.
He did only a few miracles in Nazareth, because of their unbelief.
i don't want familiarity to be the thing that keeps Him from working in me.
so i try to read Him as if for the first time, and let Him surprise me again.
